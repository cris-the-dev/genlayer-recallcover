Product recalls

Products below have been recalled by their makers or importers. Stop using them and follow the instructions given.

  • Acme T9 two-slice toasterThe Acme T9 two-slice toaster is recalled because the crumb tray can catch fire. Stop using it and contact Acme for a free replacement.
  • Lumo Kids night light, model NL-2The Lumo NL-2 night light is recalled because the plug casing can crack and expose live parts.
